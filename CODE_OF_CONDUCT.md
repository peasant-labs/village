# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community. Be respectful,
constructive, and considerate. Debate ideas and technical tradeoffs without
attacking people. Respect privacy, consent, and the sensitive nature of transcript
data.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and publishing another person's
private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through an
appropriate private GitHub channel. Do not use the vulnerability-reporting
channel for ordinary disagreements. Maintainers may remove content or restrict
participation when behavior threatens the safety or health of the community.

Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, proportionality, and the
privacy of everyone involved.
